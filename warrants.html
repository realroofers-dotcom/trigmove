The wire

Warrants and financings, as they are filed

Every filing whose text carries the language of a warrant financing — a price reset, a cashless exercise clause, an ownership blocker, an inducement. We read the documents, not the press releases, and the exhibit is where the terms live.

A match is not a finding. This language is ordinary and lawful, and most of these deals are unremarkable. A filing appears here because the document is worth reading — nothing on this page says a company or any person did anything wrong.

What the marks mean Price reset — the exercise price falls if the company later sells stock cheaper. Cashless exercise — shares issue with no cash to the company. Ownership blocker — the holder is capped below the level that would require an ownership filing.
